Which rate is this?

This calculator uses the mid-market rate published once a day by ExchangeRate-API. It is close to the "today's won–dollar rate" in the news and to the base rate (매매기준율) Korean banks announce.

Why is the bank more expensive?

Banks add a margin to the base rate, so on the same day you will see different prices:

  • Buying cash — paying won for foreign banknotes, above the base rate — around 2% for US dollars, more for less common currencies.
  • Selling cash — changing leftover notes back to won, below the base rate.
  • Sending / receiving transfers — a smaller gap than cash.

Exchange discounts in Korean banking apps, or a card with low foreign-transaction fees, get you closer to the rate shown here.

How to calculate

If 1 US dollar is 1,400 won, then 50 dollars is 50 × 1,400 = 70,000 won. Yen and Vietnamese dong are usually quoted per 100: if 100 yen is 950 won, 10,000 yen is 95,000 won.

FAQ

How often do rates change?

Real rates move all day while markets are open. This page uses a rate updated once a day, so check your bank's live rate before changing a large amount.

Is it the same as the rates box on the home page?

Yes. Both use the same data, saved once on this device, so the numbers match.

Is my amount saved?

Only the last currency and amount, in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
