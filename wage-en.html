What is weekly holiday pay?

In Korea, if you work every scheduled day in a week, you are paid for one extra day of rest. Under the Labor Standards Act this applies to part-time workers too, including at small businesses with fewer than 5 employees.

  • Your scheduled hours must be at least 15 hours a week (as set in your employment contract).
  • You must not miss any scheduled day that week. Being late or leaving early does not count as an absence.

How it is calculated

Holiday pay = (weekly hours ÷ 40) × 8 hours × hourly wage
At 40 hours a week or more, it is capped at 8 hours.

Example: 2026 minimum wage 10,320 KRW, 5 hours × 4 days

FAQ

I work 14 hours a week. Do I get holiday pay?

No. Weekly holiday pay starts at 15 scheduled hours a week.

What if I miss one day?

You lose that week's holiday pay, but you are still paid for the hours you worked. It starts again the next week you work every scheduled day.

My employer won't pay it. What can I do?

Talk to your employer first with your contract and work records. If that doesn't work, you can call the Ministry of Employment and Labor helpline (1350) or file a complaint.

Can I be paid less during probation?

On a contract of one year or more, up to 90% of the minimum wage is allowed for the first 3 months of probation — but not for simple manual jobs such as cleaning, security, kitchen help or delivery.
