What are Renju rules?
  • Black and White take turns. The first to get 5 in a row horizontally, vertically or diagonally wins. Black moves first.
  • Because moving first is an advantage, the following moves are forbidden for Black only:
    • 3-3: a move that makes two open threes at once
    • 4-4: a move that makes two fours at once
    • Overline: a move that makes 6 or more in a row
  • Black needs exactly 5 to win; White wins with 5 or more.
  • A move that makes five for Black wins even if it is also a forbidden shape. 4-3 is allowed.
  • You have 15 seconds per move. Run out of time and you lose.
