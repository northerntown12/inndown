How it is calculated

  • Monthly rate = annual rate ÷ 12 (4.5% a year → 0.375% a month)
  • Equal payments: payment = loan × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)
  • Equal principal: principal = loan ÷ n each month; interest = remaining balance × r
  • Interest-only: loan × r each month, the whole principal in the last month
  • Grace period: interest only during the grace months, then the chosen method over the remaining term

Banks may charge interest by the day or round differently, so real amounts can differ slightly. With a variable rate, payments are recalculated when the rate changes.

FAQ

How much do I save by paying early?

Interest falls with the principal you pay back. Enter your current balance and the remaining term to compare. Ask your lender about early-repayment fees.

Equal payments or equal principal?

Equal principal always costs less interest in total, but the early payments are higher, so check what your monthly budget can handle.

Is my loan information saved?

Only in this browser, so the form is filled in next time. Nothing is sent to a server.
