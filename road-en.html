How to play
  • Hold gas (↑ or Space on a PC, [Gas ▲] on a phone) to speed up; let go to coast. Brake (↓) slows you quickly.
  • The road has two lanes each way. Right of the yellow center line is your direction; the two left lanes carry oncoming traffic.
  • Use ← → (arrow buttons on a phone) to change lanes. When slow cars block both of your lanes, cross the center line to pass, dodge the oncoming cars and get back. Curves push you to the outside.
  • Hitting a car costs a life ♥. Lose them all and it's game over. Driving off the road slows you down.
  • Pass a car +20, dodge an oncoming car +10, squeeze past very close for a close call +50. Distance points are doubled while you drive in the oncoming lanes.
  • Reach the finish (12 km) for +1,000 per life left, plus a time bonus for a fast run.
  • Traffic starts light; later there are more oncoming cars and slow cars side by side, and some cars signal and change lanes. Watch for the blinking amber light!
  • 4 cars: sports car (fastest), sedan (balanced), jeep (nimble, less slowed off-road), pickup truck (sturdy, 4 lives).
