With spaces or without?

With spaces counts every space and line break as a character; without spaces counts only visible characters. Most application forms and online text boxes limit the count with spaces, so use that number unless the instructions say otherwise.

Emoji and characters built from several code points (such as flags or accented letters) are counted as one character, the way you see them.

When do bytes matter?

Older systems and text messages measure length in bytes, not characters. There are two common rules:

RuleKorean characterLatin letters, digits, spaceUsed by
Korean 2-byte (EUC-KR)2 bytes1 byteKorean text messages, older forms
UTF-83 bytes1 byteModern websites and databases

In Korea a short text message (SMS) is usually up to 80–90 bytes (40–45 Korean characters); longer messages become LMS. Limits vary by carrier and service.

FAQ

Why does another site show a slightly different count?

Sites differ in whether a line break counts as one or two characters and whether trailing spaces count. For a strict limit, paste into the actual form for a final check.

Is my text saved?

Only in this browser, so you can pick up where you left off. It is never sent to a server. On a shared computer, press "Clear" when you are done.

What does "Tidy spaces" do?

It turns runs of spaces into one, collapses three or more blank lines into one, and trims spaces at the start and end.
