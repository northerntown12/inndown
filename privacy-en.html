Privacy Policy

Records after work (the "Site") values the privacy of its visitors and has adopted this Privacy Policy in accordance with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws. If this English version differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

1. Information we collect

The Site has no sign-up or login feature and does not ask visitors to enter personal information such as names or phone numbers. However, the following information may be generated and collected automatically while you use the Site:

  • IP address, browser type, operating system, date and time of visit, pages visited, and the referring site
  • Identifiers created by cookies and similar technologies

If you contact us directly, for example by email, we may keep your email address and message in order to reply.

2. How we use it

  • To check visitor statistics and improve the Site
  • For security and to prevent misuse
  • To answer inquiries

3. Cookies

Third-party services used on the Site (such as visitor counting) may use cookies. You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings; if you refuse cookies, some features may not work properly.

4. Information stored only in your browser

Information such as your to-dos and notes, the note cover password, your weather location, sound on/off settings and unit converter choices is stored only in your browser (local storage). It is not sent to the Site operator's servers and the operator cannot see it. It is deleted when you clear the site data in your browser.

5. Location information

If you use the "My location" feature of the weather widget, and only if you allow it in your browser, your approximate location (latitude and longitude) is sent to the weather service to load the weather for that area. The operator does not separately collect or store this location information.

7. Retention and deletion

Inquiry emails received directly by the operator are deleted without delay once answered, unless the law requires them to be kept for a certain period. Automatically collected access information is kept and deleted according to each third-party service's policy.

8. Sharing with third parties

The operator does not sell or provide visitors' personal information to third parties, except where required by law.

9. Your rights

You may at any time ask to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information. Please contact us using the details below and we will act without delay.

10. Children's privacy

The Site does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.
