Privacy Policy

WowEzBox (wowezbox.com, the "Site") values the privacy of its visitors and has adopted this Privacy Policy in accordance with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws. If this English version differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

1. Information we collect

The Site has no sign-up or login feature and does not ask visitors to enter personal information such as names or phone numbers. However, the following information may be generated and collected automatically while you use the Site:

  • IP address, browser type, operating system, date and time of visit, pages visited, and the referring site
  • Identifiers created by cookies and similar technologies

If you contact us directly, for example by email, we may keep your email address and message in order to reply.

When you use online Omok or Janggi, the room code, moves, connection status and the chat messages (up to 100 characters each) you exchange with your opponent are stored on our server (Cloudflare) to run the game. We do not ask for your name or other personal details, and chats are visible only to the two players. Please do not write contact details or other personal information in the chat.

2. How we use it

  • To check visitor statistics and improve the Site
  • For security and to prevent misuse
  • To answer inquiries

3. Cookies

Third-party services used on the Site (such as visitor counting) may use cookies. You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings; if you refuse cookies, some features may not work properly.

4. Information stored only in your browser

Information such as your to-dos and notes, the note cover password, your weather location, sound on/off settings, unit converter and exchange calculator choices, the date of birth and saved D-days in the age calculator, values entered in the severance, hourly wage, loan and percent calculators, text typed into the character counter, timer, stopwatch, Pomodoro and interval settings and history, calculator history and memory, game records (Omok, 2048, Daily Word, Sudoku, Minesweeper, Janggi, Road Trip, an unfinished Janggi game, and your chosen Road Trip car), the last weather data received, and the Omok and Janggi chat on/off settings are stored only in your browser (local storage). It is not sent to the Site operator's servers and the operator cannot see it. It is deleted when you clear the site data in your browser. Timer notifications are shown only on this device, and only after you allow them with the "Turn on notifications" button; their content is not sent anywhere.

5. Location information

If you use the "My location" feature of the weather widget, and only if you allow it in your browser, your approximate location (latitude and longitude) is sent to the weather service (Open-Meteo) and to a place-name service (BigDataCloud) to load the weather and the name of that area. The operator does not separately collect or store this location information.

7. Retention and deletion

Inquiry emails received directly by the operator are deleted without delay once answered, unless the law requires them to be kept for a certain period. Automatically collected access information is kept and deleted according to each third-party service's policy. Online Omok and Janggi rooms and chat messages are deleted automatically one day (24 hours) after last use, and only the latest 40 messages are kept per room.

8. Sharing, processing by service providers, and transfers abroad

Sharing with third parties: The operator does not sell or provide visitors' personal information to third parties, except where required by law.

Processing by service providers: The operator has no separate contracts entrusting the processing of personal information to others. To run the Site, however, the operator uses the services listed in section 6, and connection information may be processed by them in the process. This includes site hosting (GitHub) and the data-relay and online-match server (Cloudflare).
