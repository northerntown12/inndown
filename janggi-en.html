How to play
  • Tap one of your pieces to see where it can go, then tap a dot. Win by checkmating the opposing General.
  • Chariot (車) any distance straight · Cannon (包) must jump exactly one piece (cannons can't jump or capture cannons) · Horse (馬) one straight + one diagonal · Elephant (象) one straight + two diagonal · General one step inside the palace · Guard (士) one step in any direction (straight or diagonal) inside the palace · Soldier (卒/兵) one step forward or sideways.
  • Horses and elephants are blocked if a point on their path is occupied. Chariots, cannons and soldiers can also move along the palace diagonals.
  • You must answer a check. When you are not in check you may pass.
  • There is no move limit. The game ends only when a General can no longer be protected (checkmate).
  • Facing Generals (bikjang) is not scored as a draw in this game.
  • 🌐 Play online: Quick match (anyone) or Create a room (send the code or link to a friend). You have 1 minute per move — run out of time and you lose. To take back a move, ask your opponent (3 times per game). You can also chat; only the two of you can see it. There is no time limit against the computer.
  • Your game is saved on this device, so you can close the window and continue later.
