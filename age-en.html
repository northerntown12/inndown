    International age, year age and Korean age

    Since June 28, 2023, Korea uses international age ("man age") for laws and official documents: you are 0 at birth and gain a year on each birthday — the same way most of the world counts.

    MethodFormulaBorn Dec 20, 2000 — as of Nov 15, 2026
    International agethis year − birth year (−1 before your birthday)25
    Year agethis year − birth year26
    Traditional Korean agethis year − birth year + 127

    A few Korean laws — such as buying alcohol and tobacco, military service and school entry — still use year age, so this calculator shows it too.

    How D-days are counted

    The target date is "D-Day"; the days before are D-1, D-2… and the days after are D+1, D+2…. If today is Nov 15 and your exam is on Nov 20, it's D-5.

    "Save this D-day" stores the name and date only in this browser. Next time you open the page the list is recalculated from today. Up to 10 can be saved; use × to delete.

    Why is the 100th day 99 days later?

    Anniversaries such as a couple's or a baby's 100th day usually count the start date as day 1. If you met on March 1, day 100 is June 8 — 99 days later. The anniversary section uses this method for days 100, 200, 1000 and yearly anniversaries.

    "N days after" in the date calculator counts the base date as day 0, so 100 days after March 1 is June 9.

    FAQ

    When does someone born on Feb 29 get a year older?

    In common years, the calculator treats March 1 as the birthday.

    Is my date of birth sent anywhere?

    No. Everything is calculated in your browser. Your date of birth and saved D-days stay in this browser's storage and are removed when you clear it.

    Which year does the zodiac use?

    It uses the solar birth year. Traditionally the zodiac changes at Lunar New Year or the start of spring, so people born in January or February may belong to the previous year's sign.
