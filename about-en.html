About

About WowEzBox and the blog What a Wonderful World~.

Welcome to WowEzBox

We use things like a calculator, exchange rates and the weather every day, yet using them often means installing an app, skipping ads or signing up first. WowEzBox started from a simple wish: "It would be nice if it just worked the moment you open it."

Everyday tools on one screen

The home screen brings together a calculator, live exchange rates, weather and air quality, postcode search, to-dos and memos, a unit converter and today's stock market. You can even drag the clock wherever you like. There are separate tool pages too, such as an age and D-day calculator, a loan interest calculator, a severance pay calculator and a character counter.

Games for a short break

Omok (Gomoku), 2048, Daily Word, Minesweeper and Janggi (Korean chess) are ready to play right in your browser, with nothing to install.

No hassle, for everyone

No sign-up and no installation needed. Memos and game records are never sent to a server; they stay only in your own browser. Whether on a computer or a phone, I want anyone to be able to use it with ease.

Stories worth sharing

Beyond the tools, I share stories through the blog "What a Wonderful World~", mostly about travel, Anglo-American culture, music, coffee and everyday life. I believe a little of life's stories alongside handy tools can turn a quick stop into a place you'll want to come back to~

Little by little

If anything feels inconvenient, or you think "I wish there were a tool for this," please let me know anytime. I'll listen to every bit and keep polishing this place~
