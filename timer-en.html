What is the Pomodoro method?

A time-management habit that started in the late 1980s, when an Italian university student timed his study sessions with a tomato-shaped kitchen timer. You focus on one task for 25 minutes, then rest for 5, and after four rounds take a longer 15–30 minute break. "Just 25 minutes" makes it easier to start, and planned breaks keep you fresh for longer.

New to it? Start with 25 / 5. If you get into the zone easily, try 50 / 10. Spend breaks stretching or drinking water rather than scrolling.

Interval workout timer

Work hard for a short burst, rest briefly, and repeat for a set number of rounds. Popular sets are 20 s work / 10 s rest × 8 rounds (4 minutes), 40 / 20 × 8 and 30 / 30 × 10. Beeps sound for the last 3 seconds of every part, and a sound plus a voice cue tells you when work and rest switch — no need to watch the screen. Adjust the intensity to how you feel.

FAQ

Will the alarm ring if I'm in another tab?

Yes. Time is measured against the moment the timer should end, so it stays accurate in the background. Turn on browser notifications to get a pop-up even while you're in another window. Browsers only allow sound after you've pressed a button on this page once.

What happens when my phone screen turns off?

With "Keep the screen on while running" checked, the screen stays on while a timer runs (in supported browsers). If the screen turns off or you switch apps, the phone may pause the page, so the alarm can be late or ring when you come back. Keep the screen on for important alarms.

Do timers disappear when I reload?

No. Timers, the stopwatch, Pomodoro and interval sessions are all remembered on this device, so they continue after a reload or reopening the page. Timers that finished while the page was closed are shown as done.

How many timers can I run?

Up to 8 at the same time. Use "Add a timer", then tap one in the list to show it on the big dial. Give it a name and the alarm will tell you which one finished.

Can it ring at a specific time?

Yes. Choose "Ring at a time" and pick the time. If that time has already passed today, it rings at that time tomorrow.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Settings, timers and Pomodoro stats are stored only in your browser. Clearing the site data removes them.

All alarm sounds are generated by this page. If you hear nothing, check your phone's silent mode and volume.
