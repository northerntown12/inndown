Why these tools

After work I kept searching for the same things — "how many square metres is 34 pyeong?", "how old am I in Korean age now?", "what is the dollar rate today?" — on different sites every time. So I built them myself and put them in one place, with no pop-ups and no sign-up, ready the moment the page opens.

  • Nothing is sent anywhere. All calculations run in your browser. Only things you will reuse, such as a birth date, D-days or memos, are kept on this device.
  • Works on phones. Every tool is sized for small screens, with input text large enough that iPhones do not zoom in.
  • Explained, not just calculated. Each tool shows the rule it uses (for example 1 pyeong = 400/121 m²) and answers common questions.
