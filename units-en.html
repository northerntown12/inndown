Pyeong ↔ square meters

The pyeong is a traditional Korean unit of area: a square 6 ja (about 1.818 m) on each side. 1 pyeong = 400/121 m² ≈ 3.3058 m², and 1 m² ≈ 0.3025 pyeong, so multiplying square meters by 0.3 gives a rough pyeong figure.

Since 2007, Korean property ads and contracts must use square meters, but people still talk about homes in pyeong in everyday conversation, so converting in your head is a common need.

Why a "34-pyeong" apartment is 84 m²

The "pyeong type" people mention is usually based on the supply area (your private area plus shared areas like stairs and hallways). That's why a unit with 84 m² of private area (about 25.4 pyeong) is often called a 34-pyeong type.

Fahrenheit ↔ Celsius

The formulas are °C = (°F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9 and °F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32. For a quick mental estimate, subtract 30 from °F and halve it — that is within 1–2 degrees for everyday temperatures.

  • 32°F = 0°C (water freezes), 212°F = 100°C (water boils)
  • 98.6°F = 37°C (body temperature), 68–77°F = 20–25°C (comfortable room)
  • −40°F = −40°C — the only point where both scales meet
  • Oven 350°F ≈ 177°C, 400°F ≈ 204°C

Inches, feet, yards, miles ↔ cm, m, km

1 inch is exactly 2.54 cm, 1 foot (12 in) is 30.48 cm, 1 yard (3 ft) is 0.9144 m, and 1 mile is 1.609344 km.

  • TV and monitor sizes measure the screen's diagonal. A 55-inch 16:9 screen is about 139.7 cm diagonally, roughly 122 cm wide and 68 cm tall.
  • Height: 5′7″ is 152.4 cm + 17.78 cm ≈ 170 cm.
  • Golf: 150 yards is about 137 m.
  • Running: 5 km ≈ 3.1 mi, a half marathon ≈ 13.1 mi, a marathon (42.195 km) ≈ 26.2 mi. 60 mph ≈ 97 km/h.

Pounds, ounces, geun ↔ kilograms, grams

1 pound is 0.45359237 kg (about 454 g) and 1 ounce, 1/16 of a pound, is about 28.35 g.

The geun is a traditional Korean weight. At Korean butcher shops 1 geun of meat is 600 g, while vegetables and fruit are sometimes counted at 375 g per geun. This converter uses the 600 g meat geun.

"fl oz" on drinks is a volume unit, not weight. One US fluid ounce is about 29.57 mL, which is different from the weight ounce (28.35 g).

Gallons ↔ liters, acres ↔ pyeong

This converter uses the US gallon (about 3.785 L), the unit for US gas prices and milk jugs. The imperial (UK) gallon is larger, about 4.546 L.

1 acre is about 4,047 m², or about 1,224 pyeong. A soccer pitch (about 7,140 m²) is roughly 1.8 acres.

FAQ

Exactly how many square meters is one pyeong?

400/121 m², or about 3.305785 m². People usually round it to 3.3 m²; conversely 1 m² ≈ 0.3025 pyeong.

Are the numbers I type sent to a server?

No. All calculations happen inside your browser. Only the name of the last unit pair you chose is saved on this device.

Why do other sites give slightly different results?

Rounding and the standard used (US vs UK gallon, meat vs vegetable geun) differ. This converter uses the exact defined values (1 in = 2.54 cm, 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg, etc.) and then rounds for display.

Is this the same as the small converter on the home page?

Yes, it's the same converter, and your choice carries over. This page just shows it larger, with reference tables and explanations.
