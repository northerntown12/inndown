Use the arrow keys (← ↑ → ↓) to merge matching numbers.

How to play
  • Slide the board with the arrow keys or your finger, and every tile moves as far as it can that way.
  • When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one (2+2=4, 4+4=8 …). Your score goes up by the merged number.
  • Each move adds a new tile (a 2, sometimes a 4) in an empty spot.
  • Make a single 2048 tile to win! You can keep going for bigger numbers.
  • The game ends when the board is full and nothing can merge. Undo works for the last move only.
  • Your game is saved on this device, so you can close the window and pick up where you left off.
