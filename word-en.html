Records after work

How to play
  • Guess the hidden 5-letter English word in six tries.
  • Type any 5 letters and press Check (or the Enter key). The tiles change color.
  • A Green: the letter is in the word and in the right spot.
  • A Yellow: the letter is in the word but in a different spot.
  • A Gray: the letter is not in the word.
  • Today is one puzzle a day, the same word for everyone (changes at midnight, Korea time). Practice as much as you like.
  • Stuck? Use 💡 Hint (3 per game: an example sentence → a letter → another letter), or 🏳️ Reveal to see the answer (counts as a loss).
  • When you finish, you'll see the word's meaning and an example. Your record is saved on this device.
