    FAQ

    In what order are things calculated?

    Brackets, then powers, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction. So 2 + 3 × 4 is 14, not 20 — old pocket calculators that work left to right may give a different answer.

    How does the % key work?

    After + or −, it uses a percentage of the previous number: "200 + 10%" adds 10% of 200, giving 220 — the same as a shop calculator. On its own, "10%" is 0.1. For discounts and VAT the percent calculator is easier.

    Where is my history saved?

    Only in your browser, up to the last 100 calculations. Nothing is sent to a server. Press "Clear history" twice to delete it all.

    Can it handle very large numbers?

    You can type up to 15 digits. Answers above a quadrillion, or very small ones, are shown in scientific notation such as 1.23e+18. Decimals are accurate to about 14 digits.
